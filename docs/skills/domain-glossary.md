# Domain glossary

## Site

A Site is a single, one-way bookable location. It can include a transport add-on, and the traveler supplies pickup, group-size, and other booking details.

## Experience

An Experience is a themed activity, such as a Stone Town day or watersports. It can chain two or more Sites into one bookable activity.

## Trip

A Trip is a dated, group-size-aware itinerary that combines Sites, Experiences, Safari, and Transport into one journey.

## Safari

A Safari is a wildlife product. Its details include parks, game-drive windows, vehicle class, and itemized park fees.

## Transport

Transport is a pre-built, external point-to-point backend. Twende calls its API to quote, create, and inspect rides; it does not recreate transport logic or its data model.
